The game

What is Play Time?

Play Time is a card game for couples, designed by a sex therapist. Roll the 12-sided die, draw a card from the matching category, read it out loud, then do it, tweak it, save it or pass. There are 72 cards across seven categories and five heat levels. You can learn it in a minute.

Who is it for?

Adult couples, 18 and over, who want more connection, more play and more variety in their sex life. It works whether you've been together six months or twenty years. It's designed for any couple, whatever your gender, orientation or relationship style. Cards talk about "you" and "your partner," not about specific bodies.

How long does a game take?

As long as you want. Cards run from five minutes to a whole evening, and the time is printed on every card. Some nights you'll play one card. Some nights you'll play ten.

Do we need anything else?

No. Everything you need is in the box. The Toy-Friendly expansion asks you to bring one toy you already own. A few cards suggest household things like a scarf, ice or a blindfold, and you can always swap them for something else.

Playing

What if one of us doesn't want to do a card?

Pass. There's no limit, no penalty and no explanation needed. Put the card back and roll again. The only rule that matters is that both of you are having a good time.

Can we change a card?

Yes, please do. If most of a card sounds great and one detail doesn't, keep the part that works. The cards are prompts, not instructions.

How do we keep it at our comfort level?

Before you play, agree on a heat ceiling, say, nothing above Heat 3. Pull any cards that are off the table, and put them in your Keep List at the back of the rulebook so they stay out. The spice meter on every card tells you where it sits before you commit.

What if we want to stop mid-card?

Stop. Not slow down, not "let me talk you into it." Either of you can end the game at any time, and no discussion is owed.

What are the rarities?

Each card is Common, Uncommon or Rare. Rarity changes how the card looks and how often you'll draw it. It never changes how extreme the card is. Rare cards are just more memorable.

Can we play it with more than two people?

It's written for two. Any other arrangement is up to you and your partners, as long as everyone involved has agreed to it.

Expansions

What are the expansions?

There are six packs of 12 cards: Date Night, Weekend Getaway, Long-Term Couples, Quick & Dirty, Toy-Friendly and Travel. You can shuffle them into the base deck by category color, or play one pack on its own for a themed night. See all six.

Do I need the base set to use them?

Yes. Expansions use the base set's die and rules.

Are the expansions more intense?

Some are. Each pack shows its heat range on the box. Long-Term Couples and Date Night are the gentlest. Quick & Dirty starts at Heat 3.
