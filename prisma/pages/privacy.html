This Privacy Policy explains how Divinity Comics Inc ("Play Time", "we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you visit playtimetcg.com, buy from our store, subscribe, or play online (together, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Play Time is an adult card game for couples sold and operated by Divinity Comics Inc, [Company address], [City, State ZIP], United States. Questions about privacy: hello@playtimetcg.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash).
  • Order details: shipping name, address and phone number, the items you buy, and your order history.
  • Subscription details: which plan you have, its status and renewal dates.
  • Messages: anything you send us through the contact form or by email, including attachments.
  • Age confirmation: your confirmation that you are 18 or older.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages viewed, actions in the online game (rooms, rolls, cards drawn), and the time and date of visits.
  • Device data: IP address, browser type, operating system and screen size.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: see section 7.

Payment information

We never see or store your card number. Payments are processed by DivinityCoin, our payment processor. DivinityCoin sends us a confirmation of payment, the amount, and a payment reference so we can fulfil your order or subscription. DivinityCoin's own privacy policy governs the information you give them.

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To process, ship and support your orders, and to deliver digital items to your account.
  • To run subscriptions: start them, renew them, and ship monthly cards.
  • To run the online game, including matching you with the partner you invite by room code.
  • To send transactional email: receipts, shipping notices, password resets and subscription notices.
  • To send product news and new-card announcements, only if you opted in. You can opt out at any time in your account settings or from any such email.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with the law, including confirming that customers are adults.
  • To understand how the Service is used so we can improve it.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • DivinityCoin, to process payments, subscriptions and refunds.
  • SendGrid (Twilio Inc), to deliver email we send you and to receive email you send us.
  • Shipping carriers, who receive your name, address and phone number to deliver physical orders. Parcels are shipped in plain packaging.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that store our database and files.
  • Analytics providers such as Google Analytics or Meta, if enabled, and only after you pass the age gate. See section 7.
  • Authorities, when required by law, subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights, our customers or the public.
  • A successor, if Divinity Comics Inc is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

Your partner in an online game room can see your display name, your Spice ceiling and the cards you draw together. They cannot see the cards you have vetoed.

5. Adults only

The Service is intended solely for adults aged 18 or older (or the age of majority where you live, if higher). We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. How long we keep information

  • Account information: for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account by contacting us; we then delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, except as described below.
  • Order and payment records: 7 years, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Game room state: rooms are kept for 90 days after they end, then deleted.
  • Support messages: 2 years.
  • Server logs: 30 days.

7. Cookies and tracking

We use a small number of cookies and browser storage:

  • pt_session — keeps you signed in (7 days). Strictly necessary.
  • pt-site-age-ok — remembers that you confirmed you are 18 or older (30 days). Strictly necessary.
  • pt-intro-seen — remembers that you have seen the intro video (1 year).
  • Browser local storage for the contents of your shopping cart on this device.

If we enable analytics (Google Analytics or the Meta pixel), those tools set their own cookies and receive your IP address and pages viewed. They load only after you pass the age gate. You can block them with your browser settings or an ad blocker without affecting the Service.

Email we send may include open and click tracking so we can confirm delivery. Most email clients let you block remote images to prevent this.

You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
